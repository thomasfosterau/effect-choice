First release: the `Choice` model of actors choosing outcomes (sealed mechanisms, sequential protocols and agents), `TieBreak` rules, and three mechanisms built on it: `HareClark` (Tasmanian and ACT single transferable vote), `BondAuction` (AOFM-style Treasury bond tenders with discriminatory or uniform pricing) and `AflDraft` (the AFL national draft with priority picks, trades and father-son and academy bid matching).
